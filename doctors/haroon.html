About

Clinical practice

Dr. Muhammad Haroon Tayyab is a Consultant General and Vascular Surgeon based in Lahore, Pakistan, with specialized training and clinical experience in the diagnosis and treatment of vascular diseases, varicose veins, arterial disorders, diabetic foot conditions and complex vascular problems.

He has completed his Fellowship in Vascular Surgery at Combined Military Hospital (CMH), Lahore, and is also an Assistant Professor at Sharif Medical & Dental College. His clinical practice combines modern vascular surgical techniques with evidence-based treatment approaches to provide comprehensive care for patients with arterial and venous diseases.

He specializes in the treatment of varicose veins, spider veins, venous ulcers, deep vein thrombosis (DVT), peripheral arterial disease, limb ischemia, diabetic foot complications and dialysis AV fistula surgery. Advanced varicose vein options include ultrasound-guided foam sclerotherapy, injection sclerotherapy, Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA) and Endovenous Laser Ablation (EVLA), guided by clinical findings and venous Doppler studies.

Alongside vascular surgery, he provides comprehensive general and laparoscopic surgical services, including gallbladder surgery, appendix surgery, hernia repair and other common surgical conditions.
